# Privacy Policy for MyAI

**Last updated: 12 August 2026**

## Summary

MyAI does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no
analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK of any kind. The
developer has no access to your conversations or any other content you create in
the app.

## What the app processes, and where

**Everything you type or attach stays on your device.** MyAI answers using
Apple's on-device foundation model, which is part of Apple Intelligence and runs
locally on your iPhone or iPad. Your prompts, attached photos, and files are not
sent to the developer or to any third-party server.

The app stores the following **on your device**:

- Chat conversations and messages, including any images you attach
- Knowledge files you create or import
- Agents and Skills you create or import
- App settings (for example creativity, sampling mode, and instructions)

## iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs the data listed above across your
own devices using **CloudKit** and **iCloud key-value storage**. This data is
held in your personal, private iCloud database. It is governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and is **not
accessible to the developer**.

To stop syncing, sign out of iCloud or disable iCloud for MyAI in the system
Settings app. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device.

## Photos and files

When you attach a photo or file, the app reads it only to include it in your
prompt to the on-device model and to display it in your conversation. MyAI uses
the system photo picker, so the app receives only the specific item you choose
and never gains access to your photo library.

## Private Cloud Compute (not enabled)

MyAI contains an optional feature that would route requests to Apple's
server-side model via Private Cloud Compute. **It is disabled in this release**
and no request is ever sent off device. Were it enabled in a future version,
requests would be handled by Apple under Private Cloud Compute's privacy
guarantees, and this policy would be updated first.

## Children

MyAI collects no data from anyone, including children.
